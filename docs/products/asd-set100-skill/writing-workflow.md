# ASD-STE100 writing and review workflow

## Establish the task and text type

Identify drafting, revision, review, or a narrow rule/word question. For document work, distinguish procedural passages, descriptive passages, and safety instructions; one document can contain all three. Resolve missing technical context when it changes the result.[^skill]

## Retrieve the applicable evidence

For writing or review, read the complete rule checklist and dictionary guide. Load detailed rule sections when their conditions, exceptions, or examples are needed, especially section 1 for technical terms, section 8 for counting, and sections 5–7 for the text type. A narrow question needs only its relevant rules or entries.[^skill]

## Preserve technical meaning

Draft or revise without changing facts, quantities, units, identifiers, conditions, action sequence, obligation, or hazard severity. Preserve mandated labels, quotations, and approved terminology. Flag conflicts and unknown facts rather than inventing instructions or making uncertain statements definite.[^skill]

## Check vocabulary in context

Review ordinary words and relevant multi-word expressions, including familiar vocabulary. Use batches of at most 20 queries and reuse earlier evidence. Match the entry's part of speech, approved meaning, permitted form, and restrictions. Check replacement words as well as original words.[^skill]

A missing entry needs base-form or technical-term investigation. Noun approval does not imply verb approval, and individually approved words do not establish an approved phrasal verb. An alternative can require a different sentence construction to preserve meaning. Consult the domain glossary when deciding whether a term qualifies as a technical noun or technical verb.[^guide]

## Review rules and deliver

Review all applicable numbered rules, including STE sentence counting, commands and conditions, descriptive paragraphs, active voice and its exception, punctuation, consistent terminology, and safety wording. Recheck vocabulary introduced by changes. Treat the general recommendations separately from numbered requirements.[^skill]

For a revision, return the revised text first, followed by material notes. For a review, identify each passage, the rule or dictionary evidence, and a suggested correction. Cite rule numbers and dictionary printed pages. Distinguish confirmed findings, context-dependent terminology, and unresolved questions; incomplete checks do not justify claiming complete compliance.[^skill]

## Long documents

Work section by section with a short shared terminology list and an unresolved-issue list. Check terminology across sections after revision. Retrieve references once where practical and avoid repeating large extracts in progress messages or the final answer.[^skill]
